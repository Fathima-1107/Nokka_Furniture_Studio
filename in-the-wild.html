The making

What you're paying for, before it becomes a chair.

1

Rough stock

Kiln-dried walnut and ash, sorted by grain before a single cut is made.

2

Joinery

Mortise-and-tenon and dovetail joints, cut and fitted by hand — no screws, no brackets.

3

Finishing

Hand-rubbed oil finish, three coats, cured over a week before a piece ships.

Built to be fixed
50 yrs

Every joint is designed to be taken apart and reglued, not thrown out. If a piece comes back to us — for a wobble, a scratch, a re-oil — we repair it at cost, for as long as we're standing.
