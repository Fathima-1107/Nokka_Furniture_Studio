The full catalogue.

Small batches, built in the order they're ordered. No warehouse, no overstock — just the queue.

Five materials. No substitutes.

We source in full logs and bolts, not sheets — so what you touch is what grew.

Walnut American black walnut, oil-finished, deepens with age.
White Ash Straight-grained, springy — used where flex matters.
Brass Solid, unlacquered — takes on a living patina.
Bouclé Wool Undyed, woven in small lots by a mill outside Boras.
Travertine Quarried in single slabs, honed rather than polished.

From log to living room.

Four stages, none of them rushed. A chair takes eleven weeks, start to finish.

01
Selection

Boards are picked and matched for grain before a single cut is made — the difference is invisible until it isn't.

02
Joinery

Mortise-and-tenon and dovetail joints, cut and fit by hand. No screws touch the visible frame.

03
Finishing

Hardwax oil, hand-rubbed in three coats. It wears the way skin tans, not the way paint chips.

04
Inspection

Every piece is sat in, leaned on, and signed by the joiner who built it before it ships.
