Our Philosophy

Built to Outlive the Room

NOKKA is a small joinery studio based on Gothenburg's industrial waterfront. Established in 2016, we reject modern flat-pack production in favor of heirloom-quality woodworking.

True Solid Timber

We build exclusively from solid black walnut, ash, and European oak logs. You won't find wood veneers, particleboard, or plastic dowels in our shop. Solid timber handles wood movement naturally, expanding and contracting with seasonal humidity rather than splitting or warping.

Craftsman Joinery

Our frames are joined using traditional blind tenons and wedged dovetail joints. These structural wood joints hold together under friction and timber pins, meaning there are no screws or metal fasteners hidden in the core frame to strip over time.

"We measure our products not in warranty cycles, but in generations. A chair should grow old, take on a patina, and be passed down."
— Nils Andersson, Studio Lead
